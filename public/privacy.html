Privacy Policy

AR FleetTrack

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

AR FleetTrack ("we", "our", or "the application") is a logistics management platform engineered to assist fleet owners and operators in tracking trips, freight receivables, driver payables and roadside claims, vehicle operating expenses, and overall fleet profitability. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, cloud database storage, usage, security, and your user rights under applicable data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

AR FleetTrack collects and processes only the information necessary to provide logistics management, accounting, and operational reporting:

  • Operational Fleet Data: Trip records, route information, vehicle registrations, contracted freight rents, TDS deductions, client company receivables, driver salary payouts, roadside expense claims, reimbursable expenses, and operating expense receipts (fuel, toll, maintenance). This data is securely stored and managed in our application's protected cloud database infrastructure to ensure persistent availability, integrity, and reliable multi-device access for your operations.
  • User Account & Profile Details: User credentials, profile configurations, and entity master records (trucks, drivers, and client companies) entered to organize and partition your fleet accounting.
  • Google User Data (Optional Integration): If you choose to enable automated expense synchronization with Google, AR FleetTrack connects via Google OAuth 2.0 to access your Gmail inbox in strictly read-only mode (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly).

3. How We Use Google User Data

When you enable Google integration, AR FleetTrack uses read-only access exclusively for:

  • Scanning incoming transaction alerts strictly to detect logistical operational debits (such as FASTag toll alerts, fleet card fuel purchase notifications, and maintenance receipts).
  • Extracting transaction amounts and dates, and automatically tagging them to the matching vehicle in your fleet dashboard.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure: AR FleetTrack's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage, Security & Sharing

  • No Data Selling or Advertising: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, logistics operational records, or email contents to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers under any circumstances.
  • Database Security: All data stored in our cloud database infrastructure is protected using industry-standard security protocols, encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and secure access controls to guard your confidential commercial records against unauthorized access.
  • No Third-Party Ad Tracking: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, behavioural trackers, or data-broker analytics inside the application.

5. User Rights & Data Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

We believe that fleet owners should always maintain complete ownership and sovereignty over their business data:

  • Data Erasure / Blank Slate Reset: In compliance with the statutory Right to Erasure, AR FleetTrack provides a dedicated Clear Data (Privacy Reset) tool accessible in your Settings tab and dashboard footer.
  • Permanent Deletion: You can permanently wipe your operational records (trips, operating expenses, driver claims, company settlements) or execute a full entity wipe to reset to a clean blank slate if you decide to discontinue using the application.
  • Accidental Loss Prevention: All data erasure actions require explicit dual-confirmation through an interactive confirmation dialog ("Are you sure you want to permanently delete?") to ensure your records are never cleared unintentionally.

6. Revoking Permissions

You can disconnect your Google account from AR FleetTrack at any time directly through the app interface in the Master Management tab, or directly via your Google Account Security Settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
